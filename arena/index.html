MOVE

Run, jump, experiment. WASD / arrows move. Mouse looks after entering; drag the right side on touch. Space jumps; press again in flight for a double jump. Click or F fires.

Four roaming target bots respawn when hit. This is a movement sandbox with simple targets, not a competitive game.

Mutator controls: C / Shift holds hover (up to 7 seconds). C + Space superjumps. Double-tap a movement key to dodge; near a wall, dodge toward it and keep holding to wall-run. Jump off to flip. B toggles bullet time (requires 75 adrenaline to start).

Classic inspired: ground dodge, ordinary jump and an early double jump. Original UT2004 physics, maps, assets, weapons, networking, and the actual mutator are not loaded. Constants are approximations. *Touch: hold Hover then Jump for superjump.

Original C → WebAssembly movement core. Serve this folder over HTTP; see README.md for reproducible build and tests.
